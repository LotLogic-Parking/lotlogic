4. SMS Program Terms — Tow Dispatch Alerts

Program name: LotLogic Tow Dispatch.

Description: LotLogic sends transactional SMS/MMS dispatch alerts to contracted towing enforcement partners when our cameras detect unauthorized parking at a property they service. Messages contain the property name and address, license plate, timestamp, vehicle photo, and any applicable pass history.

Opt-in: Enforcement partners provide their mobile number and consent to receive dispatch alerts as part of a signed service agreement with LotLogic. No public or consumer sign-up.

Message frequency: Message frequency varies and is driven by real-time violations at the properties you service. Typical range is 0–10 messages per day per recipient.

Message and data rates: Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier.

Support contact: For help, email lotlogicai@gmail.com or reply HELP to any message.

HELP — Reply HELP to any LotLogic message to receive support contact information.

STOP — Reply STOP to any LotLogic message to opt out of dispatch alerts. You will receive a final confirmation and no further messages. To resume, notify LotLogic in writing.
