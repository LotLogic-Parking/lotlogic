Common questions

Before you sign.

What does the property pay?

Nothing, at any step — and there's no minimum volume. The property receives 10% of enforcement revenue generated on its lot.

What do we need to provide?

Nothing. No power, internet, permits or staff time.

How does everyone get registered?

Your office's vehicle list, a registration link your office sends, and QR signs at each entrance. Your office approves each request or turns on auto-approval.

What about someone parking for a few hours?

They scan the QR sign and enter their plate and the unit they're there for. The short-stay pass expires on its own.

What if enforcement happens by mistake?

Fees are refunded and any damage is repaired at no cost to the property. Refer the person to us.

Do you integrate with AppFolio?

No. Your roster is seeded from your office's export and kept current in the LotLogic dashboard.

Ready to hand off your parking?

See the live system in 15 minutes. We'll send the agreement and every certificate of insurance.
