Apartments & associations

Your community parks with a pass, new arrivals register by QR, and your office stays out of it. Signed on day 0, enforcing on day 8.

$0 to the property10% of enforcement revenue to you30 days' notice to end

  2. Days 1–7Registration & install. Passes loaded from your list, a link you send, and QR signs. Cameras go up only where you approved. No enforcement this week.
