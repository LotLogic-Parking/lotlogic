How it works

We handle everything upstream. You handle the job.

01

Cameras on the lot

Solar, cellular plate-reading cameras at partner properties, installed and maintained by LotLogic.

02

Passes by QR

Everyone who's allowed to park registers a parking pass. The system knows who belongs.

03

Verified on site

A flag is checked against the live record — passes, pending requests, holds — before anything is sent.

04

You get the job

Plate, vehicle, location and photos land on your phone. You roll once, to the right car.
