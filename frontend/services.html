Camera setups

Two ways to cover a lot. Same platform behind both.

At the gate

Tripwire

Two cameras at each parking entrance read every plate in and out, with a photo and timestamp. Cars with a parking pass are recognized; anything else gets a quiet 15 minutes to register before it's flagged.

  • Every entry and exit recorded with plate, time and photo
  • Short stops that leave inside the window close on their own
  • About 30 minutes to install per entrance

Best for apartment communities, associations and gated lots.

Across the lot

EagleEye

Cameras placed around an open lot read plates as cars arrive, settle and stay — no single entry point needed. Built for lots with several driveways or none at all.

  • Covers open lots, strip centers and multi-entrance properties
  • Time limits and stay limits you set per lot
  • Same evidence trail and dashboard as Tripwire

Best for commercial lots, truck parking and multi-entrance properties.

How it works

From a plate read to a resolved violation.

The cameras flag. People verify. Nothing is enforced that your property didn't choose.

01

Plate read

A camera reads the plate on the way in and saves the photo and time.

02

Pass check

The plate is checked against your parking passes, pending requests and holds. New arrivals get 15 minutes to register by QR.

03

Verified on site

A flag goes to a LotLogic representative, who checks the live record on site before anything happens. Every check is logged.

04

Enforcement

Towing by N Style Towing, our licensed partner, on call 24 hours. Booting by LotLogic agents is also available.
